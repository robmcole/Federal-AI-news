## Congress & Legislation

**[Pentagon's $29.5B AI Arsenal plan caught in reconciliation funding uncertainty](https://insidedefense.com/ai-news/pentagons-ai-arsenal-plan-caught-uncertainty-over-reconciliation-funding)** (2 minute read)

DoD's FY2027 budget request includes $29.5 billion for its AI Arsenal initiative, a government-owned AI supercomputing network of hardened data centers and advanced GPUs. Inside Defense reports the plan is among the defense investments with an uncertain future as Republicans weigh one more budget reconciliation bill before Democrats take control of Congress in January. If reconciliation stalls, the money would need to survive regular appropriations under a Democratic majority that has signaled tougher oversight of Pentagon tech deals. The rest of the story is paywalled, so details beyond the topline aren't public.

*Source: Inside Defense · Reliability Score: 4/5*

**[Rep. Bill Foster drafts a bill for Fed open-source "real-time regulation" demos, including AI risk monitoring](https://fedscoop.com/federal-reserve-real-time-data-bill-foster/)** (4 minute read)

Foster (D-Ill.), ranking member of the House Financial Institutions subcommittee, released a discussion draft of the Real-Time Regulatory Study Act. It would have the Federal Reserve Board build and test open-source modules for regulatory dashboards, automated bank and credit union reporting, stress testing, AI risk monitoring and automated fraud and AML screening, using no live customer accounts. Modules would be published with security and maintenance plans, with a systemwide test within 18 months, an interim report to Congress within 180 days and a final assessment within two years. It's an early marker of the RegTech and AI-oversight agenda Democrats may bring to financial regulators in 2027.

*Source: FedScoop · Reliability Score: 4/5*

## Acquisition & Contracts

**[Navy opens $99M small-business competition to run Flank Speed cloud and network operations](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/navy-flank-speed-cloud-network-operations-solicitation)** (4 minute read)

NAVSUP FLC Norfolk posted a solicitation Monday for a small-business IDIQ worth up to $99 million, with three to six awards, to support Naval Network Warfare Command. The scope covers Azure IL5 and IL6 tenants, Microsoft 365, Intune, Entra ID and Conditional Access, Defender policies, SD-WAN, records management and eDiscovery. Offerors need an active Top Secret facility clearance at submission, and most of the 16 labor categories require TS. Proposals are due Dec. 11, ordering runs January 2027 through December 2031, and awardees must sign associate contractor agreements with each other. It's a sizable cleared-cloud ops opportunity as the Navy moves its classified network to a zero-trust, cloud-centric model.

*Source: GovConWire · Reliability Score: 4/5*

## Defense & National Security

**[Air Force rolls out 100/200/300-level AI courses and AI tools for 15 mission areas](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/air-force/2026/11/air-force-previews-ai-training-as-it-prepares-to-deploy-new-tools/)** (3 minute read)

Department of the Air Force Chief Data and AI Officer Susan Davenport said the Air Force and Space Force will launch AI tools for 15 missions, from intelligence to nuclear operations, each overseen by a named "domain principal." A three-tier AI curriculum arrives by the end of November: 100-level for all airmen and guardians, 200-level for building AI workflows (including legal guardrails) and 300-level for developers. Completions will go into permanent training records and can count toward promotions. Quarterly showcases track progress, with the third set for December or January and R&D labs and industry invited, which gives vendors a clearer route to mission owners.

*Source: Federal News Network · Reliability Score: 4/5*

## Civilian Agency Initiatives

**[VA.gov relaunches with LLM-powered plain-language search and passkey sign-in](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/technology-main/2026/11/va-gov-refresh-is-early-step-in-broader-push-to-modernize-digital-services/)** (4 minute read)

VA Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary for IT Zack Schwartz said the new VA.gov opens with an AI search modeled on America.gov's homepage. It uses large language models to route veterans' plain-language, typo-filled questions to the right service. The refresh, shaped by 90,000 survey responses, adds passkeys and "magic link" one-time sign-in for checking claim status, alongside Login.gov, ID.me and the new CLEAR option. Separately, Deputy Secretary Paul Lawrence said the EHR rollout is 10% complete and on track for 170 sites by 2031. It's one of the most visible federal deployments of generative AI search to date.

*Source: Federal News Network · Reliability Score: 4/5*

**[DOE's AI-FORTS program advances AI cyber defense for the energy grid](https://www.executivegov.com/articles/doe-ai-forts-ai-cybersecurity-critical-infrastructure)** (4 minute read)

DOE's CESER office runs Artificial Intelligence for Operationally Resilient Technologies and Systems (AI-FORTS), launched in 2024. The program focuses on three areas: defending energy infrastructure from AI-enabled threats, using AI for cyber defense, and securing AI deployed in energy systems. Its Sandia-built C2E2 project cut energy-system data prep from about two months to hours and detected and located cyber threats with 95% accuracy, and the team will next try LLM-generated impact assessments. DOE's FY2027 budget justification says it will shift cybersecurity research toward AI, a signal for lab partners and grid-security vendors.

*Source: ExecutiveGov (summarizing GovCIO Media & Research) · Reliability Score: 3/5*

## Industry & Vendor News

**[Primordial Labs raises $18M to scale its natural-language command-and-control software](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/10/primordial-labs-seriesa-anura-ai)** (2 minute read) ⚠️

Primordial Labs' Series A was led by Ballistic Ventures, with Squadra Ventures and Lockheed Martin Ventures participating. Its Anura interface turns plain-English voice commands into actions on more than a dozen crewed and uncrewed military systems, from drone altitude changes to re-pointing armored-vehicle sensors. The company has a master teaming agreement with General Dynamics Land Systems and plans offices in Detroit and Huntsville. It's a sign that voice and LLM interfaces for human-machine teaming are drawing both defense-prime and venture money. The funding details come from the company.

*Source: Axios · Reliability Score: 4/5*
