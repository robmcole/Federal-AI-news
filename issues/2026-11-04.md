## Congress & Legislation

**[PATIENT AI Act Draft Seeks Discussion on Payment Rules for Healthcare AI](https://www.executivegov.com/articles/senate-patient-ai-act-healthcare-ai-discussion-draft)** (4 minute read)
Senate HELP Chairman Bill Cassidy released a discussion draft of the PATIENT AI Act, which would draw a line between AI health products overseen by FDA and those overseen by HHS's health IT office (ONC), so higher-risk tools get tougher review and lower-risk ones reach patients faster. It would also let FDA review AI software updates on a rolling basis, direct HHS to expand AI in its own operations with privacy and security guidance, and create a payment pathway to encourage adoption. Comments are open by email through Dec. 1. It's the most detailed congressional blueprint yet for how health AI gets regulated and reimbursed, and it would shape the market for vendors selling into CMS, VA and HHS.

## Acquisition & Contracts

**[FedRAMP 20x picks up steam with first full 'Class C' certification to AI company](https://fedscoop.com/fedramp-20x-first-full-class-c-certification-ai-company/)** (4 minute read)
Perplexity is the first company certified under the full, sped-up FedRAMP 20x process at "Class C," the level closest to the old "moderate" baseline and the most common in the marketplace, and the first certification compliant with the FedRAMP Consolidated Rules for 2026. The certification lets GSA and USDA use Perplexity Enterprise and its API platform. FedRAMP now counts seven Class A certifications and 12 Class B pilots (including Google Gemini for Government), while OpenAI's ChatGPT holds a pilot Class C, and nearly 100 products, including some from Anthropic and Cisco, are in the pipeline. It's the clearest sign yet that 20x is moving from pilots to real authorizations, which should shorten the path for AI tools into agencies.
*Source: FedScoop · Reliability Score: 4/5*

**[Everforth ECS Awarded $200M NGA Canyonlands GEOINT AI Contract Extension](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/everforth-ecs-nga-geoint-ai-contract-extension-canyonlands)** (3 minute read)
The National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency gave Everforth ECS (formerly ECS) a two-year extension worth more than $200 million on Canyonlands, its effort to move geospatial-intelligence AI from development into operational use. The work covers program management, software engineering, systems integration, training and user adoption, and follows a $104.3 million sole-source base award in September 2025 that carried over work from the Army's $190 million Project Runway. The extension roughly triples NGA's investment in the program and signals that the intelligence community is funding AI adoption, not just prototypes.
*Source: GovConWire · Reliability Score: 4/5*

**[Judge peels apart protests against DHA's $1.6B health readiness award](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/contracts/2026/11/judge-peels-apart-protests-against-dhas-16b-health-readiness-award/417002/)** (4 minute read)
Court of Federal Claims Judge Zachary Somers denied protests by LTS Health Partners and incumbent QTC Medical Services (a Leidos subsidiary) against the Defense Health Agency's $1.6 billion Remote Health Readiness Program-4 award to OptumServe; the ruling was issued Oct. 9 and unsealed Oct. 28. The protesters raised 17 arguments over about 150 pages, which Somers said amounted to "their best impression of a lint roller," and he found no prejudicial error, even though DHA's source selection council had recommended QTC. The opinion is a pointed warning to protesters to develop a few strong arguments rather than throwing everything at the court.
*Source: Washington Technology · Reliability Score: 4/5*
*Primary document: [Court of Federal Claims ruling](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/media/general/2026/11/qtc_dha_denial_ruling_20261028.pdf)*

**[SSN(X) Concept Design Expression of Interest](https://fedops.com/opportunities/566c81fb3da641dea5c77de557f2b904)** (2 minute read)
NAVSEA's SSN(X) program office (PMS 351) posted a pre-solicitation notice (N0013527R6276) for an upcoming Other Transaction competition seeking industry concepts that use "Super Intelligence" to design the SSN(X) attack submarine's internal system arrangement and reduce the risk of fitting it into the whole-ship design. Traditional and non-traditional contractors can send a short, non-proprietary expression of interest by email by 12:00 a.m. ET on Nov. 6, and the formal Area of Interest notice could post as early as next week. It's a rare case of the Navy asking for AI to help design a major warship rather than just run its systems.
*Source: FedOps copy of the SAM.gov notice · Reliability Score: 3/5*

## Defense & National Security

**[Pentagon Orders Review of Commercial AI, Cloud on Classified Networks](https://www.meritalk.com/articles/pentagon-orders-review-of-commercial-ai-cloud-on-classified-networks/)** (3 minute read)
In an Oct. 27 memo, Under Secretary for Research and Engineering Emil Michael directed the Science, Technology and Innovation Board's Permanent Subcommittee on National Security Innovation to study how commercial AI and cloud services can be brought onto classified networks. The study, required by Section 907 of the FY2026 NDAA, will look at facility, network and vendor-access requirements, whether common security authorizations could work across the department, whether current acquisition rules support buying commercial services, and how the Pentagon should organize its digital engineering and software delivery. The outcome could reshape how frontier AI vendors get onto secret and top-secret networks, which has been one of the biggest bottlenecks for defense AI.
*Source: MeriTalk · Reliability Score: 4/5*
*Primary document: [STIB study listing](https://stib.cto.mil/current-efforts/)*

**[Super Intelligence takes center stage at SSC's 2026 Fight Tonight finalists' pitch](https://www.ssc.spaceforce.mil/Newsroom/Article-Display/Article/4638929/super-intelligence-takes-center-stage-at-sscs-2026-fight-tonight-finalists-pitch)** (6 minute read)
Space Systems Command said eight AI-focused finalists pitched senior Space Force leaders for up to $12 million in Fight Tonight funding, with SpaceWERX possibly backing SBIR projects too. The projects include TEMPO for launch range scheduling at Vandenberg (Metronome), an SI-enabled network overlay with Hughes, the DAISSE SATCOM status engine, Orbital Sentinel with Nyla and Microsoft, ATWOOD targeting with LMI, Mission Fabric logistics with XRDNA, and the ORION adversarial space simulator with HII. Winners will be announced Nov. 13 at the Space Force Ball, which will show which space AI concepts the service plans to fund near term.
*Source: Space Systems Command · Reliability Score: 5/5*

## Industry & Vendor News

**[Former Army boss Dan Driscoll joins OpenAI as senior counselor](https://defensescoop.com/2026/11/03/dan-driscoll-openai-strategic-counselor/)** (3 minute read)
Dan Driscoll, who resigned as Army secretary in August after reportedly clashing with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, joined OpenAI in a newly created role as senior counselor for national security modernization. He'll advise OpenAI leadership and partners on using AI for national and cyber security, with a focus on U.S. allies, and help the company understand what defense institutions need. The hire deepens OpenAI's defense push after its $200 million CDAO prototype deal and the addition of ChatGPT models to GenAI.mil.
*Source: DefenseScoop · Reliability Score: 4/5*
*Primary document: [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/dan-driscoll-joins-openai/)*

**[Copilot for US Government delivers advanced models, app integration](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/publicsectorblog/copilot-for-us-government-delivers-advanced-models-app-integration/4569928)** (4 minute read) ⚠️
Microsoft says OpenAI's GPT-5.6 is now available to Microsoft 365 Copilot users in its GCC, GCC High and DoD clouds, closing the gap with commercial customers. The November update also brings "Edit with Copilot" in Word, Excel and PowerPoint to all three government clouds, Copilot in Forms, and meeting scheduling from Copilot chat (available in GCC, rolling out to DoD). For agencies on Microsoft 365, it means frontier-class models inside tools workers already use, without leaving government-authorized environments.
*Source: Microsoft (vendor announcement) · Reliability Score: 3/5*
